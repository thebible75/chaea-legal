# Acceptable Use Policy

This Policy outlines recommended and prohibited usage of ChaeA Chat to keep the experience safe and responsible.

## 1. Recommended Use
- Connect only to Ollama servers you own or are explicitly authorized to use
- Enable HTTPS / VPN / authentication when exposing servers externally
- Treat model responses as reference material, never as absolute fact

## 2. Prohibited Use
The App must not be used for:

- Unauthorized collection or processing of personal data, medical records, financial information, or other sensitive data
- Content that infringes copyright, trademark, or other intellectual property rights
- Content that promotes discrimination, hate, violence, or illegal activity
- Content inappropriate for minors
- Generating text/code for fraud, phishing, or malware
- Information gathering for unauthorized intrusion, hacking, or privilege escalation
- Automation tools, bots, or scripts that interfere with the normal operation of the App

## 3. Responsibility for Model Responses
1. Responses shown in the App are generated by the LLM running on the Ollama server you connect to.
2. Models can produce inaccurate or hallucinated outputs.
3. Decisions in specialized domains (medical, legal, financial) must always be verified with a qualified professional.
4. The user is responsible for the consequences of acting on model responses.

## 4. Responsibility for Content
1. Messages and attachments you provide are sent to the Ollama server you have registered.
2. How that server handles the data depends on its operator's policy.
3. You are responsible for ensuring your content does not infringe on the rights of third parties.

## 5. What This App Does Not Do
- Operate its own backend server
- Collect, store, or transmit user data to the App developer
- Run advertising, analytics, or tracking
- Identify users (no account system)

## 6. Reporting
If you encounter rights violations or policy violations, please contact:
- incoad@gmail.com

## 7. Policy Changes
This Policy may be updated alongside app updates. Changes are reflected on this screen.
